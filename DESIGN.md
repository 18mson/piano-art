# Piano Art - Design Direction

## Identity & Character
- **Product**: Top-down grand piano instrument and falling-notes music player.
- **Soul**: A refined, tactile acoustic concert grand piano meets modern studio workstation. Clean, focused, and purposeful.
- **Anti-Slop Filter**: Eliminates generic AI cyber tropes (no full-page purple/cyan gradients, no floating glowing orbs, no Sparkles/Magic icons, no decorative em dashes, no forced monospace on body copy, no fake terminal windows).

## Three Dials
- **ENERGY: 2 (Balanced)** — Clear contrast, tactile controls, restrained and purposeful accent colors.
- **RHYTHM: 2 (Consistent & Purposeful)** — Clear grouping of instrument geometry, player controls, and playback aids.
- **MOTION: 1 (Calm)** — Smooth, functional transitions for modals and active key states; no unnecessary pulsing or floating animations.

## Palette
- **Canvas / Space**: Ebony `#090d16`, deep slate `#0f172a`, border slate `#334155`.
- **Keys**: Classic ivory `#f8fafc` (white keys) and matte ebony `#1e293b` (black keys).
- **Accents**:
  - Warm Brass / Amber (`#f59e0b`) for Middle C (C4) and active alerts.
  - Studio Sky / Azure (`#0284c7` / `#38bdf8`) for selected controls and active playhead.
  - Studio Emerald (`#10b981`) for sustain pedal on state.

## Typography
- **Primary**: Sans-serif (`system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`) for all UI labels, buttons, dialogs, and navigation.
- **Data / Notation**: Monospace (`ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, monospace`) used strictly for musical notations: pitch codes (`C4`, `A0`), BPM (`128 BPM`), and timestamps (`01:24`).
